Pack opening

LOM · card engine

One card, one pack. The sequence is a state machine in src/pack.js; every visual response lives in src/pack.css and keys off data-phase, so timing and look are tuned separately. The card's art is preloaded and decoded while the pack is still sealed, which is why the turn-over never shows an empty frame. Reduced motion skips the theatre and goes straight to the revealed card.

Stats on this card are the illustrative sample from samples/cards.js, not derived from match data.
