League of Managers · card engine v0.1

Card Foundry

Five player cards, one per rarity band, rendered by the engine rather than drawn by hand. Every card is built from data plus a rarity theme — frame, foil treatment, glare strength and artwork are all derived, so a card for any of the thousands of player-seasons in the catalogue comes out of the same code path.

Placeholder stats. The player-seasons, photos and champion art are real; the ratings, champion pools and season lines are illustrative values chosen to exercise the five treatments. Production cards are minted by the data pipeline and carry their real source and game counts on the back.

Photos are not cleared for release. Leaguepedia tags player headshots Copyright fairuse, which is a claim about the wiki's use, not a licence granted onward. Fine for a private league; not fine for anything public. The champion-art path below needs no clearance.

Drag across a card to tilt it. Tap to flip.

    Rarity drives the treatment

    One theme object per band sets the frame stack, accent, glow and foil type. Rarity itself comes from rarityForOvr(ovr, accolades) — Icon needs the 92+ band and a Worlds title or MVP, which is why it is a short list.

    Two art paths, one slot

    Each card here carries that season's headshot over a blurred splash of their signature champion — Faker's is his 2016 Spring photo, not a current one. A card with no photo on file falls back to the champion art alone, which is what most of the catalogue will do and needs no clearance.

    The 3D is CSS, not WebGL

    A perspective wrapper, translateZ parallax on the art and the OVR badge, and pointer-driven custom properties feeding the foil and glare layers. Ten elements, no library, and it stays smooth on a phone.

    // one card, anywhere
import { createCard } from '@lom/card-engine';

const { stage } = createCard({
  cardKey: 'faker-2016', playerName: 'Faker', season: 2016,
  team: 'SKT T1', region: 'LCK', role: 'MID', ovr: 97,
  accolades: ['WORLDS_WINNER'],       // -> ICON
  subStats: { laning: 94, teamfight: 96, /* ... */ },
  championPool: [{ champion: 'Ryze', games: 34, mastery: 97 }],
});
document.body.appendChild(stage);
